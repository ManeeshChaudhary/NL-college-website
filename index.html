Welcome to N.L. Inter College

Learning with purpose.
Growing with character.

At N.L. Inter College, education is a meaningful journey—one that develops knowledge, confidence and a strong sense of responsibility. Since 2000, our school has been committed to offering students an encouraging place to learn, discover their strengths and prepare for the opportunities ahead.

Rooted in the values of the N.L. Family, we bring academic learning and personal growth together in a supportive school community in Vighaipur, Sasni.

Admission Information

  • Admissions available for Nursery to Class 12, subject to availability.
  • Senior secondary streams exclude Commerce.
  • Contact the office for current dates, criteria and class vacancies.

Documents to Prepare

  • Completed admission form
  • Previous school transfer certificate, where applicable
  • Recent photographs of the student
  • Identity and date-of-birth documents as requested
  • Previous academic records, where applicable

Important Instructions

  • Submit accurate details and readable document copies.
  • Keep original documents available for verification.
  • Admission is confirmed only after school approval.
  • Call the school before visiting to confirm office availability.
